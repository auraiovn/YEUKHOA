Transcript

AURA starts from a simple idea: online fashion should give you enough information to make a confident decision. We combine clear product pages with optional browser based Virtual Try On. The camera view helps you compare colour, scale and proportion, while measurements and material notes remain the source for sizing. In stores, the same decision logic can connect to AURA Smart Mirror. The goal is not more technology. The goal is a clearer path from seeing a product to feeling ready to buy it.
